## Open decisions and follow-ups (owner)

1. G06: wire anonymisation and retention, or confirm the manual process; decide on the customer row and sessions on deletion; PII masking scope without `customers.view_pii`.
2. Content role: edits to already-published activities go live without publish rights, and categories it creates are published immediately. Confirm whether that is intended.
3. readonly/finance hold `inquiries.view_all` but agent list routes require `view_own` (they get 403). Decide the intended matrix.
4. Remaining client-supplied snapshot fields on inquiry items; unknown `variantId` priced at base.
5. Restrict activity `video` URLs to `https:` with a data check (stored rows may contain other schemes).
6. Storefront CSP nonce/hash prototype (see the plan).
7. Convert `paidAt` rejects zoneless `datetime-local` (functional, not security).
8. Off Vercel (no trusted client IP), `inquiry.service` and admin login apply no IP limit (phone/email limits and the login lockout still apply), while OTP and lookup share one bucket. Not an issue on Vercel; decide before any non-Vercel deployment.
9. Import rollback restores content but not publish status: rolling back a batch applied with "publish" over an existing draft leaves the listing published with its old content (pre-existing behaviour; `products.edit` can already edit live listings). Record and restore status per item if this matters.
10. Lower-priority review notes: the per-instance analytics ceiling can be filled by one client off Vercel; the console Revert button is shown to holders who will get 403; a CSV cell of a space then `'=1` round-trips to `=1`.
